In the Point of Sale, unavailable attribute values are greyed out in the
product configurator and a combination containing one of them cannot be
added to the order.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

The Point of Sale works offline: a change of availability is taken into
account when the Point of Sale is reloaded, not in real time on an
opened session.
